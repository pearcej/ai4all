Every story has a beginning. Today’s AI systems may seem as though they appeared almost overnight, but their story stretches back much further than ChatGPT—or even the Internet. The ideas behind artificial intelligence have developed over decades through periods of excitement, disappointment, and breakthroughs. Understanding AI’s back story helps us understand why today’s systems can accomplish so much, while also recognizing the challenges they have yet to overcome.
