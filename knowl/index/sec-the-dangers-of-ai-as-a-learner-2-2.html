The core danger is that generative AI tools are highly optimized to automate output, which frequently encourages a shift from active learning to passive task completion. When a student uses AI to bypass the underlying mental effort of an assignment, they experience a form of cognitive surrender, the act of outsourcing one’s critical thinking to an external source and bypassing the mental work of learning. While the technology allows users to navigate information quickly, relying on it to generate immediate answers removes the necessary cognitive investment required to build durable human expertise.
