Biases

The bias is a measure of how easy it is to get the neuron to output a 1. To put it in more biological terms, the bias is a measure of how easy it is to get the artificial neuron to fire. For a neuron with a really big bias, it’s extremely easy to output a 1. But if the bias is very negative, then it’s difficult for the neuron to fire. The bias serves as an internal threshold, determining how much total weighted evidence a neuron must accumulate before it shifts its state.
