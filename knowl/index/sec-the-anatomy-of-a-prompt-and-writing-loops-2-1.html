In the context of large language models (LLMs)—artificial intelligence systems designed to understand and generate human-like text—prompting refers to the method of providing an LLM with a specific input or cue to generate a desired output or perform a task. Prompting is crucial because it directly influences how effectively these models understand and respond to user queries. A well-crafted prompt can guide an LLM to generate more accurate, relevant, and contextually appropriate responses.
