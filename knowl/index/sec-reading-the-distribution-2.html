Graphing data is the first and often most important step in data analysis. In this day of computers, researchers all too often see only the results of complex computer analyses without ever taking a close look at the data themselves. This is all the more unfortunate because computers can create many types of graphs quickly and easily. This section covers some effective ways of graphing distributions (a mathematical function that describes how the possible values of a variable are spread out and how frequently they occur).
